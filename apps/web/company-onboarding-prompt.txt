## Company Onboarding Enhancement (Substantial Feature)

### Identity Section
- Legal company name
- Registration number (CIN or equivalent)
- Date of incorporation
- Country and jurisdiction
- Registered address
- Operating address
- Website
- Company logo upload

### People Section
- Key people with LinkedIn profiles (dynamic, add with + button)

### Documentation Section
- Legal documents (upload)
- KYC records of key people (upload)

### Financials Section
- Audited financial records (upload)
- Patent documents (upload)
- Environmental certifications - ESG compliance (upload)

All data goes to admin for verification.

### Post-Onboarding: Ask Investment
Once onboarding is complete, company dashboard shows "Ask Investment" button.
Company fills in:
- Funding round (Seed / Series A / etc.)
- Amount raising (INR)
- Minimum investment (INR)
- Valuation (INR)
- Equity offered (%)

### Notes
- Use + button for LinkedIn profiles (as many as needed)
- All uploads stored server-side
- Admin sees all this data in the review queue
- Fundraising details shown to investors once approved

## Fundraising & Fractional Investment System

### Core Flow
1. Company fills "Ask Investment" form — equity auto-calculated from valuation + raising amount
2. Active fundraising round appears on company's public page (visible to all investors)
3. Investors can submit an offer (standard) or counter-offer (different equity %)
4. Counter-offers show on company dashboard — company accepts/rejects each
5. Every accepted investor gets fractional equity: (their_amount / total_raising) * equity_offered
6. Investor dashboard: remove the contracts section entirely

### Auto-calculated Equity
equity_percent = (amountRaising / valuation) * 100  — shown as read-only field

### InvestorOffer model (new)
- fundraisingRequestId, investorEmail, investorName
- amountINR, counterEquityPercent (null = accepts stated terms)
- status: pending | accepted | rejected
- sharesPercent: set on acceptance = (amountINR / totalRaising) * equityOffered

### FundraisingRequest updates
- Add amountRaisedINR (tracks total collected)
- offers InvestorOffer[]

### APIs needed
- GET /api/fundraising?startupId=X
- POST /api/fundraising/[id]/offer
- GET /api/fundraising/[id]/offers
- PATCH /api/fundraising/[id]/offers/[offerId]

### UI changes
- Company public page: fundraising round card in overview tab with Invest button
- Company dashboard: incoming offers list with accept/reject
- Investor dashboard: remove contracts/portfolio section, show their own offers
